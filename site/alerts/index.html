Severe weather alerts

Get a notification on this device when severe weather affects the places you choose in Western Australia.

On iPhone or iPad: add this page to your Home Screen first

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari (the square with an arrow).
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open WWXW from your Home Screen and come back to this page.

Apple only allows website alerts from apps added to the Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or later).

Your places (up to 3)

A postcode alerts for its main town or suburb. Tick "anywhere in this postcode" for a farm or station that spreads across a big rural postcode — you'll get more alerts.

Alert me about

Daily storm outlook A morning alert (about 8:20 am) when the West WX Watchers outlook has your place at this level or higher today.

These are unofficial alerts from West WX Watchers and may be delayed. The Bureau of Meteorology issues the official warnings — check bom.gov.au and Emergency WA, and follow the advice of emergency services.

Privacy: we keep only what's needed to send your alerts — this device's alert address, your places (rounded to about 1 km) and your choices. Nothing else is collected, and "Turn off" stops all alerts to this device.
