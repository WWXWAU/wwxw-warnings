About this forecast

Spraying combines Delta T and wind. Good: Delta T 2–8 °C and wind 3–15 km/h. Marginal: Delta T 8–10 °C or wind 15–20 km/h. Stop: Delta T over 10 °C or wind over 20 km/h. Inversion risk: Delta T under 2 °C or wind under 3 km/h, when spray drift can travel a long way; surface inversions are common from late afternoon until after sunrise.

Delta T is the air temperature minus the wet-bulb temperature: the higher it is, the faster droplets evaporate.

Wind is the forecast at 10 m. Near the ground it is usually a little lighter.

Frost uses the forecast overnight low (6 pm to 9 am) and a rough grass temperature: on clear, calm nights the ground runs 2–4 °C colder than the air in a weather screen. Possible: grass 2 °C or below. Likely: 0 °C or below. Severe: −2 °C or below. Low-lying paddocks can be colder still.

Fog is flagged when the models have the air close to saturation (temperature within about 1 °C of the dew point) with light winds overnight or early morning, or forecast visibility under 1 km.

Evapotranspiration is reference ET (FAO-56, short crop): the water a well-watered pasture loses to the air in a day. Yesterday's measured ET at DPIRD's stations is on Live Obs.

The forecast comes from computer models on a 0.5° grid (about 50 km), updated four times a day. Always check the conditions on the paddock before and while spraying, and follow the label.
