Weather balloons

The latest Bureau of Meteorology balloon flights over WA, drawn as Skew-T diagrams.

▬ temperature ▬ dew point ┄ lifted parcel (from the ground) LCL cloud base · LFC free convection · EL storm tops ■ CAPE ■ CIN

Data: Bureau of Meteorology radiosonde flights, from the WMO network via the University of Wyoming upper-air archive. Balloons go up at about 7 am and 7 pm WST (not every station every time). The lifted parcel starts from the surface reading at launch, so a morning flight usually shows less CAPE than the afternoon will have.
