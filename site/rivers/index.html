Every gauge

River levels from the Department of Water and Environmental Regulation's telemetered gauges: automatic, unchecked readings, shown for general interest. Also the Bureau of Meteorology's flood-warning gauges run by other agencies, the Bureau's flood levels, and rain from DWER and Bureau gauges. Roads affected by flooding from Main Roads WA (CC BY 4.0, Commissioner of Main Roads); check the Travel Map before you go. Flow (m³/s) from DWER's hourly discharge reports. Rain upstream uses DWER's hydrographic subcatchments to find the rain gauges in each river's catchment. Gauge positions and the first week of history from the Bureau of Meteorology's Water Data Online. Levels are metres above each gauge's zero, so they can't be compared between rivers. Checked every 2 minutes and updated as soon as DWER posts new readings. Not a flood warning service: for flood warnings go to bom.gov.au.
