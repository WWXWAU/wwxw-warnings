How the towns are rated

Each day's daytime weather (9 am to 5 pm) is scored from the Bureau of Meteorology's town forecasts, with computer-model forecasts filling in wind, humidity and the sea breeze. The best days top out at 25–29 °C, sunny, dry and with light winds.

Heat, cold, sticky humidity, wind, showers, storms and thick cloud take points off. On hot days, a coastal town gets credit when the sea breeze (the Doctor) is due by early afternoon and stops the heat climbing.

Inland, warm and still days carry a rough fly warning and lose a few points.
