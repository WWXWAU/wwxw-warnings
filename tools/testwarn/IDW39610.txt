Flood Warning for the Fitzroy River

Issued at 2:15 pm WST on Friday 20 November 2026
by the Bureau of Meteorology, Perth.

Moderate flooding is occurring along the Fitzroy River at Fitzroy Crossing, with minor flooding expected downstream at Willare and Looma.

Heavy rainfall over the past 24 hours has caused river levels to rise.

The next warning will be issued by 8:00 pm WST Friday.
